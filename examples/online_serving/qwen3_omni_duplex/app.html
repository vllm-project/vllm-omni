How a turn flows

Hold talk and speak. Audio streams to the server as input_audio_buffer.append at 200 ms. On release the client sends input_audio_buffer.commit.

This adapter holds audio until commit, then hands the thinker one complete turn. Qwen3-Omni has no learned listen/speak token, so it cannot decide mid-utterance whether you have finished — the client decides.

Barge in cancels the in-flight response and bumps the session epoch. Stale audio is dropped, the session and its KV survive.
