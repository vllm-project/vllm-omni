# Assembly and review

Use current clip revisions in shot-table order. Preserve useful dialogue and SFX;
mix a continuous requested score underneath them. Inspect edits at sound bridges
and dialogue boundaries, avoiding cut vowels and accidental audio gaps. Only add
subtitles, music, or retiming consistent with the brief.

Review identity, landmarks, screen direction, light/time of day, prop state,
emotional payoff, intelligibility, SFX timing, and music balance. Look for leaked
storyboard panels, sketch lines, arrows, labels, timecodes, or watermarks. Verify
the complete file's duration, dimensions, fps, frame count, audio, and decode.

Keep planning documents, references, raw clips, and final edits in distinguishable
folders. Record the exact assets used in the final edit. After a character, scene,
prompt, clip, or score is revised, update its downstream dependents and archive the
previous result; do not silently combine stale and current versions.

Report observed defects and what was repaired, including external editing or
upscaling. A successful API response proves completion, not visual correctness.
